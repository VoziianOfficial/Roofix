Homeowner questions

Ask better questions before comparing estimates.

The right questions can make roofing provider comparison clearer. Use them before choosing who should work on your home.

01

Is the estimate itemized?

Ask for clear separation between materials, labor, disposal, repairs, and extras.

02

What roof materials are included?

Compare shingles, panels, underlayment, flashing, vents, and accessories.

03

Is tear-off and disposal included?

Confirm whether old roofing removal, disposal, and cleanup are part of the scope.

04

Are permits needed?

Permit needs vary by location and project scope. Verify this directly with providers.

05

What warranty terms apply?

Review manufacturer coverage, labor warranties, exclusions, and registration details.

06

What should the homeowner verify?

Verify licensing, insurance, estimate detail, timeline, warranty, and provider communication.
