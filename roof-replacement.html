Estimate clarity

Replacement estimates need careful review.

Replacement quotes can differ by tear-off, disposal, decking repairs, material grade, flashing, ventilation, permits, cleanup, timeline, and warranty terms.

01

Is tear-off included?

Ask whether the old roof will be removed, how many layers are included, and how disposal is priced or handled.

02

How is decking damage handled?

Clarify how damaged sheathing, soft spots, or hidden issues are identified, documented, priced, and approved.

03

What materials are specified?

Compare shingles or panels, underlayment, flashing, ridge vents, drip edge, fasteners, and accessories.

04

What warranty terms apply?

Verify manufacturer warranty, labor warranty, exclusions, registration, ventilation requirements, and transferability.

Roofix does not set provider pricing, warranty terms, timelines, permits, or estimate scope. Homeowners verify all replacement details directly with providers.
