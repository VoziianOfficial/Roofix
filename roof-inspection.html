What to ask

Inspection requests should define what you need to know.

Before choosing an inspection provider, clarify whether the review covers shingles, flashing, vents, valleys, roof edges, attic signs, drainage, and visible decking concerns.

Ask how findings are delivered: photos, written notes, severity levels, maintenance recommendations, or separate repair and replacement discussions.

Roofix does not inspect roofs, diagnose roof conditions, guarantee findings, or manage provider recommendations. Homeowners verify inspection details directly.

Roof type considerations

Inspection questions can change by roof type.

Asphalt shingles, metal roofing, flat roof systems, and tile roofing can have different inspection points, maintenance needs, and warranty questions.
