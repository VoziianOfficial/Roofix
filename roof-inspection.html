What to ask

Inspection requests should define what you need to know.

Before choosing an inspection provider, ask how the roof will be reviewed, what the output includes, and whether follow-up repair or replacement discussions are separate.

01

What areas are inspected?

Ask whether shingles, flashing, vents, gutters, roof edges, valleys, penetrations, attic signs, and visible decking concerns are reviewed.

02

Will photos or notes be provided?

Clarify whether you receive images, a checklist, written notes, severity levels, or maintenance recommendations.

03

Is it a general review or storm assessment?

Make sure the inspection type matches your goal, especially after wind, hail, debris, leaks, or recent severe weather.

04

What happens after the inspection?

Ask whether repair estimates, replacement recommendations, maintenance planning, or follow-up visits are separate services.
