Platform role

Clear role. Clear boundaries.

Roofix is designed around responsible comparison language, so homeowners understand what the platform can help with and what must be verified directly with independent providers.

What Roofix helps with

  • Organize roofing service categories.
  • Help homeowners start provider comparison.
  • Encourage direct verification.
  • Keep roof types inside the service decision flow.

What Roofix does not do

  • Does not install roofs directly.
  • Does not replace or repair roofs directly.
  • Does not inspect or consult directly.
  • Does not guarantee provider work, pricing, permits, timelines, or availability.

Roofix is an independent provider-matching platform. Roofing companies are independent and handle their own estimates, timelines, warranties, pricing, permits, and project work. Roofix does not perform roofing work directly.
