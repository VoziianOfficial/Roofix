Provider comparison factors

Compare repair providers by diagnosis and scope.

A repair estimate should explain how the issue was identified, what area is included, what may remain outside the repair scope, and whether the solution is temporary, maintenance-based, or longer-term.

01

Leak source

Ask how the provider identifies the actual leak source before recommending repair work.

02

Repair boundary

Clarify whether the estimate covers only the visible issue or nearby affected areas too.

03

Temporary vs permanent

Ask whether the proposed fix is temporary, maintenance-based, or intended as a longer-term repair.

04

Future replacement risk

Discuss whether the repair is enough or whether broader roof age may affect future decisions.

What to ask

Repair requests should explain the issue and the boundary.

Before comparing repair providers, describe the visible issue, leak location, storm exposure, missing shingles, flashing concerns, and any urgent interior signs.

Ask whether the provider is estimating a localized repair, a temporary fix, or a concern that may require a broader roof discussion.
