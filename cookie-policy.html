What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, including local storage, may store information in your browser to remember preferences or support website functionality.

Cookies and local storage do not usually identify a person by name on their own, but they may be connected with browser, device, preference, or website usage information.

How Roofix Uses Cookies

Roofix may use cookies or similar technologies for limited website-related purposes, including:

  • Remembering cookie banner preferences.
  • Supporting basic website functionality.
  • Improving page layout, usability, and performance.
  • Understanding general website usage patterns.
  • Helping maintain security, debugging, and website reliability.

This website may use localStorage to remember whether you accepted or declined the cookie banner. This helps prevent the banner from appearing repeatedly after a choice has been made.

Cookie banner storage The cookie preference key is controlled in the website configuration file. In the current setup, the key is managed through config.js.

Managing Cookies

You can usually manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Browser tools may allow you to block cookies, clear local storage, remove stored preferences, or receive alerts before cookies are stored.

If you clear your browser storage, the cookie banner may appear again because the website no longer has your previous banner choice.
