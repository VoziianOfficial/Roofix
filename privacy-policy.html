Introduction

Roofix is an independent roofing provider-matching platform. This website helps homeowners organize comparison requests related to roof installation, roof replacement, roof repair, and roof inspection or consultation.

By using this website, you understand that Roofix does not perform roofing work directly. Independent roofing providers handle their own estimates, pricing, schedules, permits, warranties, communication, and project work.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you choose to submit through forms, links, phone interactions, or email communication. This information may include:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Your phone number and email address.
  • Your project ZIP code or general service location.
  • Your selected roofing service path.
  • Details you provide about your roof, timeline, urgency, or project goal.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type, and basic website usage data.

How We Use Information

Information submitted through this website may be used to help organize roofing provider comparison requests, improve website experience, respond to inquiries, and support communication related to your request.

  • To understand your roofing comparison goal.
  • To help organize your request by service type and location.
  • To communicate with you about your inquiry.
  • To improve website performance, layout, usability, and content clarity.
  • To maintain legal, security, and operational records where appropriate.

Provider Matching

When you submit a request, information may be used to help connect or organize communication with independent roofing provider options. Providers are independent and are responsible for their own business practices, estimates, pricing, schedules, permits, warranties, and project work.

Homeowners should verify licensing, insurance, estimate scope, materials, labor, timeline, permit requirements, warranty terms, and project details directly with each provider before making a decision.

This website may use cookies or local storage to remember cookie banner preferences, support basic functionality, improve performance, and understand general website usage.

Cookie banner storage The website may use localStorage to remember whether a visitor accepted or declined the cookie banner. The storage key is managed through the website config.

Data Sharing

We may share information where reasonably necessary to support provider comparison requests, website operations, service providers, security, analytics, legal compliance, or business administration.

Roofix does not control how independent roofing providers handle their own communications, estimates, pricing, project terms, or customer records. You should review any provider’s own privacy practices before sharing sensitive information directly with them.
